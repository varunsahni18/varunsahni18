About the project

CabinAI brings driver monitoring and cabin interaction into one working demo. Camera frames produce face and hand landmarks, which feed drowsiness heuristics and gesture recognition. A shared event bus connects that state to voice requests, local knowledge retrieval, and a driving simulator. Known cabin commands take a deterministic path; an explicit routing policy sends more complex questions to a language model. I built and presented the prototype as a solo SparQ 2026 entry, winning the Jury Award among 166 teams.

What I built

  • Processed MediaPipe face and hand landmarks in the browser to estimate eye closure, blink activity, head movement, and recognized gestures.
  • Connected browser events to a Flask backend and streamed updates through server-sent events, with local command handling before model routing.
  • Added BGE-small ONNX retrieval over a 31-document corpus, keyword fallback, and separate local and cloud response paths. Fatigue forecasting remained experimental.
