About the project

Campus Navigation explores how route guidance can appear within an augmented-reality view. The prototype presents campus wayfinding through a spatial interface, connecting navigation instructions to the surrounding scene. The walkthrough follows the guidance flow as implemented in the demo; campus-wide positioning and route coverage were not validated.

What I built

  • Built a prototype interface for presenting campus-navigation guidance in AR.
  • Explored the relationship between route instructions and their position in a spatial view.
  • Recorded the navigation flow to show the guidance as implemented.
