About the project

During DAAD-supported research at TU Braunschweig, our team explored how mixed reality could make life-cycle assessment data easier to inspect. The HoloLens application places an e-bicycle and its assessment information in a spatial interface. Users can navigate the model, select product scenarios, and compare carbon emissions across the product lifetime. I worked on the application and interaction flow with collaborators at TU Braunschweig and BITS Pilani, then demonstrated the experience to a scientific jury. The work focused on presenting assessment data and scenario comparisons through spatial interaction.

What I built

  • Built HoloLens interactions for navigating the product model and selecting life-cycle assessment scenarios.
  • Connected scenario choices to views of carbon emissions over the product lifetime.
  • Collaborated across TU Braunschweig and BITS Pilani and presented the application to a scientific jury.
